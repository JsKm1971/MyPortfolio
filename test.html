RUNNING CASE

Project Lighthouse

You will use this case every week. Read it now; you will keep building on it through the capstone.

The Project

Brightwater Retail, a fast-growing online and in-store retailer, has been selected as the preferred provider for a major anchor-client contract. Final award is contingent on Brightwater committing to the capacity and go-live requirements in the proposed agreement.

To serve the client, Brightwater has launched Project Lighthouse: design, build, and commission a new automated distribution center.

◆
Your Role You are the Project Manager.

Key Facts

$48M

Project Budget

Brightwater must balance a major capital investment against demanding client requirements.

Months to Go-Live

Accept an aggressive 18-month, penalty-backed date or negotiate 22 months and risk losing the client to a competitor.

6

Major Scope Areas

Construction, automation, WMS, systems integration, workforce preparation, and phased go-live.

During Week 1, focus on understanding the uncertainty surrounding Project Lighthouse. Consider the potential threats and opportunities, the different attitudes toward risk among stakeholders, and how much risk Brightwater should be willing to accept.

You are not yet expected to develop a formal risk register, score individual risks, or perform quantitative risk analysis. Those activities will be introduced later in the course.

LOOKING AHEAD

What Is a Risk Register?

A risk register is used to document and track identified uncertainties that could affect a project. Risks can represent threats that may negatively affect objectives or opportunities that could benefit the project.
