Kick Off Meeting

The course development journey begins with a collaborative kick-off meeting that establishes expectations, responsibilities, timelines, and overall direction.

  • Introduce the development team and subject matter experts.
  • Review course goals, outcomes, scope, and timeline.
  • Identify key milestones, deliverables, and responsibilities.
  • Establish communication and feedback expectations.
