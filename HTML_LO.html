HTML accordions are an effective design strategy for organizing large amounts of content into manageable, easy-to-navigate sections. Rather than requiring learners to scroll through lengthy pages of information, accordions allow users to expand only the topics they wish to explore, reducing cognitive overload and improving the overall learning experience. This progressive disclosure approach helps maintain focus, enhances usability, and makes complex content feel more approachable. In addition to their functional benefits, accordions create a clean, modern interface that improves the visual appeal of a course while supporting responsive and accessible web design principles.

**Project Lighthouse – Interactive Project Management Case Study**

Project Lighthouse is an interactive, scenario-based learning experience designed to help students apply project management and risk management principles to a realistic business challenge. Centered on a $48 million automated distribution center project for Brightwater Retail, the case places learners in the role of Project Manager, where they must evaluate competing priorities, identify potential risks, analyze project constraints, and make informed decisions. Developed using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, the learning object features interactive accordions, responsive layouts, structured project information, and accessible design elements aligned with WCAG 2.1 Level AA guidelines. Its progressive disclosure approach allows students to explore project scope, key stakeholders, known pressures, and weekly objectives while reducing cognitive overload. Designed as a reusable case study across multiple course modules, Project Lighthouse promotes critical thinking, problem-solving, and practical application of project management concepts.

Project Lighthouse

The Project

Brightwater Retail, a fast-growing online and in-store retailer, has been selected as the preferred provider for a major anchor-client contract. Final award is contingent on Brightwater committing to the capacity and go-live requirements in the proposed agreement.

To serve the client, Brightwater has launched Project Lighthouse: design, build, and commission a new automated distribution center.

Week 1 Focus Understanding the uncertainty surrounding Project Lighthouse. +

During Week 1, focus on understanding the uncertainty surrounding Project Lighthouse. Consider the potential threats and opportunities, the different attitudes toward risk among stakeholders, and how much risk Brightwater should be willing to accept.

You are not yet expected to develop a formal risk register, score individual risks, or perform quantitative risk analysis. Those activities will be introduced later in the course.

LOOKING AHEAD

What Is a Risk Register?

A risk register is used to document and track identified uncertainties that could affect a project. Risks can represent threats that may negatively affect objectives or opportunities that could benefit the project.
