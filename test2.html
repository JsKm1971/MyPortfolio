This Week: Build the Risk Framework

This week, your job is to create a practical framework that will help the team identify and evaluate risks systematically.

Your Risk Breakdown Structure and rating scales will be used again in later weeks, so keep them clear, consistent, and usable.

Risk Breakdown Structure (RBS)

Develop an organized structure for grouping potential Project Lighthouse risks by source or category.

Probability Scale

Establish a consistent scale for describing how likely a risk is to occur.

Cost-Impact Scale

Establish clear criteria for evaluating how strongly a risk could affect project cost.

Schedule-Impact Scale

Establish clear criteria for evaluating how strongly a risk could affect the Project Lighthouse schedule.
