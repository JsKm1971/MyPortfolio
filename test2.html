Flip Cards

Hover Over Card To Reveal Answer

Q1. A team adds a final inspection step to catch defects before units ship. Is that quality assurance or quality control?
Answer: Quality control—it detects defects in the output after the work is done.
Q2. Writing a quality plan with peer-review checkpoints into the schedule is an example of which?
Answer: Quality assurance—it builds quality in to prevent defects before they occur.
Q3. True or false: quality assurance is reactive and quality control is proactive.
Answer: False—it is the reverse. QA is proactive (prevention); QC is reactive (detection).
Q4. A sponsor says testing is costing too much. Which concept helps you frame that trade-off?
Answer: Cost of quality—weighing prevention and appraisal costs against the cost of failures.
Q5. What does SIPOC stand for?
Answer: Suppliers, Inputs, Process, Outputs, Customers.
Q6. Turning stakeholder interviews into concrete acceptance criteria captures what?
Answer: The Voice of the Customer (VOC).
Q7. A "ship it and fix it later" mindset across an organization is an example of what shaping quality?
Answer: Organizational culture.
Q8. The recipient who receives and judges your deliverable—internal or external—is called what?
Answer: The customer.
