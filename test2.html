Project Lighthouse Week 2 Case

You are still the project manager for Project Lighthouse, Brightwater Retail’s $48 million automated distribution center. Following the Week 1 decision, Brightwater is moving forward with a conditional 18-month path supported by stronger risk controls. The formal risk-identification work has not yet occurred, and the initial risk register has not been created.

This week, your job is to create a practical framework that will help the team identify risks systematically. Your RBS and rating scales will be used again in later weeks, so keep them clear and usable.

Case Continuity — Project Lighthouse

Week 1: You established the risk foundations and evaluated the 18-month versus 22-month schedule decision. Brightwater is moving forward on the conditional 18-month path with stronger risk controls.

Week 2: You will create the RBS and probability, cost-impact, and schedule-impact scales before formal risk identification begins.

Looking Ahead: In Week 3, you will use the RBS to identify and document ten Project Lighthouse risks. In Week 4, you will use the rating scales to score and prioritize that same register.

Do not identify or score individual risks yet. This week is about building the framework that later risk work will use.
